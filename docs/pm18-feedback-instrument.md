## Three proposed tasks, to run only on a legitimate verified reviewer artifact
- **T1 — Source-rights comprehension:** Without attempting an external download, distinguish permitted `psychneuro-synthetic-v0` from six disabled OpenNeuro metadata entries. Success: participant explains why a public license metadata field is not proof of permitted secondary use or appropriate label semantics.
- **T2 — Scientific split and feature audit:** Find the independent unit of analysis, group membership evidence, two pre-response features and held-out test policy. Success: participant identifies independent *participants* rather than trial rows and explains why latency/correctness cannot be predictors.
- **T3 — Interpreting a hypothetical report:** Read a clearly labeled fictional-simulation report, identify null/undefined metrics and distinguish across-seed nominal interval from human population uncertainty. Use only a report generated by a **real authenticated CI artifact** after candidate validation; until then T3 is **UNAVAILABLE**.

## Short, non-leading questions
1. Role in this review (optional, select domain only: student, methods/ML researcher, research software engineer, other; no demographics requested).
2. What do you think this software is intended to do? Free text.
3. Before seeing any scores, which sources do you believe may be used? What made this clear or unclear?
4. Did you find the train/validation/test independence unit? What was it?
5. Can you find which input variables are allowed and *why* response-time/target features are excluded?
6. Where would you check whether held-out metrics are undefined or eligible? Was the reason visible?
7. In your own words, what does the interval across five synthetic-seed repeats **not** establish?
8. Were controls and errors understandable and keyboard-accessible on your actual device? Give a concrete step, not a general rating.
9. What was the most confusing or high-risk step? A reproducible example is useful.
10. Which **one** improvement would make this more useful for your actual research workflow? Why?
11. Optional: Was the task completed? yes / partly / no / not attempted; if not, where did it fail?

Avoid interpreting positive Likert ratings as scientific effectiveness. Pilot usability feedback is qualitative and convenience-sampled; no population-level efficacy conclusion.
