## P1-081 — Leakage, inference, uncertainty, metric audit
**Inspected implementation (not executed on this branch):**
- `split.py` derives seeded train/validation/test memberships grouped by `participant_id` (not rows/sessions), validates disjoint subjects, full row coverage, source hash, assignment digest and deterministic replay. At a 24-fictional-subject fixture, the split yields **14 training, 5 validation, 5 test subjects**; 480 dependent trials must not be called 480 independent subjects.
- `baselines.py` allowlists only *pre-response* `difficulty` and `stimulus_condition`, not `correct`, `response`, latency or participant ID. `StandardScaler` and `OneHotEncoder` are constructed *inside* a fresh sklearn estimator pipeline per train fold.
- `experiments.py` uses `GroupKFold(5)` inside training groups, selects dummy-prior vs logistic regression on training-CV balanced accuracy and ties favor dummy, freezes choice before comparing test outcomes; training-only final fit; validation is diagnostic and not used for selection. Both models' held-out scores are produced for prespecified comparison, **not** permission to select the better model from test scores later.
- `metrics.py` reports balanced accuracy, macro F1, 2x2 integer confusion matrix and per-class support; null/undefined for empty or single-class truth. Pooled *trial*-weighted metrics may give a heavier contribution to participants with more trials. Independent group count and variability need prominence before external inference.
- `uncertainty.py` calculates a **nominal approximate Student-t interval** after 5 eligible independently seeded *synthetic cohort repeats*, not a CI on real participants or a causal effect. Small repeat count, possible non-Gaussian bounded metrics, and synthetic RNG dependence make nominal coverage unestablished. Deliberately unbounded endpoints can lie outside [0,1] and need interpretation. Held-out test of five synthetic participants cannot support robust generalization claims; cluster-resampling a real cohort has NOT been done.
- `pipeline.py` predeclares repeated generator/split seeds and reports all eligible/ineligible runs. Fixed source and model configuration help prevent cherry-picking but **do not immunize against repeated model revisions informed by the same holdout**.

**Scientific risk register / acceptance tests for release:** (1) execute source-authored `tests/test_splits.py`, `test_experiments.py`, `test_uncertainty.py`, `test_pm08_experiments.py` and all regression tests in hosted CI on **exact reviewed SHA**; (2) injected same-participant cross-fold and malicious swapped row mapping must fail; (3) prohibited post-response features and train/test preprocessing contamination must fail; (4) exact fold hashes, group membership, versioned config, data/report artifact hashes reproducible across supported runtimes; (5) single-class CV/validation/test result must be **ineligible / null**, no hidden resplit; (6) compare handcrafted balanced accuracy/F1/confusion and subject-level summaries against fixed external reference calculations; (7) verify prospective locked model choice and no post-holdout adaptation; (8) distinguish seed-based descriptive interval from population/group-level uncertainty and quantify limitations. Standard methods: [scikit-learn grouped CV](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.GroupKFold.html), [leakage prevention](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html). **Status:** static source evidence, not passed tests or scientific validation.
