# GA-08B — official university entry, fees and funding: Germany / Sweden / Finland

**Review date:** 2026-10-19. **Career calendar queue:** 2026-10-22 (advance preparation; no learner attendance claimed). **Scope:** bachelor-level education for a credible AI/computational neuroscience or biological-science pathway. This is a jurisdiction-level and programme-level **evidence matrix**, **not a personal admission/visa/scholarship determination**. Personal nationality, immigration category, completed school award, grades, institution recognition, age, language qualifications and budget are **UNKNOWN**.

## Source hierarchy and acceptance criteria

1. Official university **programme admissions and tuition pages** determine individual route and price. National admissions services and DAAD provide generic background; their country-specific qualification mapping **cannot be imputed** without a real school document.
2. A pass requires one concrete verifiable bachelor's programme per country with official eligibility page, fee evidence, funding type, observed source date and explicit evidence gap. A passing source comparison is **not** an acceptance or financing outcome.
3. No applications, contact, paid services, credential uploads, invented certificates, or implicit citizenship/permit inference.
