## GA-09A — Three-case research ethics/rights matrix

1. **Truly invented synthetic trials:** No actual human research participant or prospective consent; test privacy by rejecting sensitive identifiers, prove generator provenance; do not infer a licence to distribute outputs from the source code's Apache-2.0 licence. The internal [registry](../../data/registry.json) says synthetic exports not separately licensed. Public data reuse is still an open decision, issue #9.
2. **Secondary recorded real human neural observations:** Even if metadata and published data appear open, verify precise data/reuse terms, participant consent for secondary purpose, de-identification, data minimization, local review and access controls. All six external OpenNeuro entries remain disabled. Permission to cite metadata is not automatic permission to ingest/redistribute recordings.
3. **Prospective volunteer measurement:** Before invitations or data acquisition check ethically valid voluntary consent, withdrawal, privacy/reidentification, secure retention, institutional/legal review, age/guardian requirements where relevant, risk/benefit and proper authorship/AI disclosure. NOT authorized to recruit or collect.

Primary ethical reference: [NIH BRAIN 2.0 neuroethics](https://www.braininitiative.nih.gov/vision/nih-brain-initiative-reports/brain-20-neuroethics-enabling-and-enhancing-neuroscience) discusses purpose-limited consent, secondary use and inferable identity. This is an educational decision table, not certified IRB/legal clearance.

## GA-10A — Side-by-side study critique

Hodgkin/Huxley: macroscopic electrophysiology of squid axon, model-based estimation; validation is degree to which voltage/time conductance equations account for observed action potentials. Neher/Sakmann: novel low-noise recording of putative elementary acetylcholine receptor-current events in denervated frog muscle. Confounding considerations differ: species/preparation temperature and model non-identifiability vs electrode noise/recording resolution/channel attribution. Measurements are not the same unit, so no statistical meta-analysis or direct replication is justified. Exact figure numbers, replicate counts, apparatus specifics and uncertainty extraction remain TO VERIFY from full figures. Counterexample: fitting membrane conductance does not directly observe each microscopic opening, and single-current traces do not by themselves establish a full propagating axon model.
